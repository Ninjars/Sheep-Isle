# Sheep Isle

Sheep Isle is a calm desktop-companion experience centred on a floating island and a bounded flock of sheep.

## Language

**Sheep Isle**:
The calm desktop-companion product centred on a floating island and its sheep.

**Desktop companion**:
The player-facing experience that coexists with other desktop applications and does not require continuous attention.

**Island**:
The floating in-world habitat presented by the desktop companion.

**Flock**:
The bounded group of sheep living on the island.
